# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a welcoming experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

We expect all community members to:
- Be respectful and inclusive
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully
- Focus on what is best for the community
- Show empathy towards others

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces and when representing the project in public spaces.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1, available at https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html

## Contact

For questions or to report issues, please open a [GitHub Issue](https://github.com/Kiara-01-Lab/vibe-doc/issues).
